Wildfire Prediction and Response

An ensemble with confidence-gated risk levels, resource allocation under scarcity and champion/challenger retraining, behind a Flask dashboard.

CompletePatent: Published, application 202641083188

The problem

Fire services need a risk level they can act on and a way to allocate scarce crews, not just a probability.

How it works

  1. weather + location
  2. RF / GB / LR ensemble
  3. confidence gate
  4. risk level
  5. resource allocation
  • Trains on the UCI Forest Fires data with the patent disclosure's own experimental setup, and reports cross-validated results instead of the disclosure's projected figures.
  • Monotonic constraints guarantee that hotter, drier or windier weather can never lower the predicted risk. Before them, a simulated heatwave scored lower than a cool wet day in 11 of 12 months.
  • Evacuation advisories are drafted automatically but wait for human sign-off, and simulated data can never retrain the model.
